# Demonstrate the actual product

Live site: https://flamingo.houston-groq-proxy.workers.dev/

## 1. A question becomes a source trail

Ask the Research guide: **BASS-II fan calibration airflow**. The guide searches evidence and offers known investigation destinations with original excerpts and source locators. Open PSI-25 to inspect its source context. A calibration question may be answered by workbook or document context; do not force it into a comparison of unrelated test rows.

The guide uses the existing limited API. Respect a displayed rate-limit wait; do not repeatedly submit the same request. `node research/evaluate_retrieval.mjs` is the offline fallback for demonstrating recorded retrieval behavior, clearly labeled as a replay.

## 2. Explore beyond investigation cards

Open Explore. Switch between Everything, Source rows, Investigations and Publication references. Search for **SIBAL** or **PSI-98**. The collection exposes 25 projects, 2,009 nonblank source rows and 364 references, rather than presenting the two curated PDFs as the whole database.

## 3. Compare source conditions without losing qualifiers

Open Saffire-I (PSI-98), mark its first two source rows and choose Compare original rows. The original `Percent O2` values must remain:

- S1: `21.5 - 21.7`
- S2: `~ 21.5`

Use Only differences and export CSV. This shows data preservation and a contextual comparison workflow; it does not establish a causal airflow effect or a validated safe oxygen level.

## 4. Discover the larger original collection

Open Files & coverage on an investigation. Inspect remote-reference counts, declared size, indexed fragments and the NASA source link. Explain the difference between indexed inspectable evidence and remote media available at the original repository. Do not say that all 12.3 TB were processed.

## 5. Show accessible learning entry points

Open Learn. Select a part of the orbital module, conceptual lunar base or rover and follow a related investigation. Try the illustrated flame controls or the six-choice science mission. These activities build curiosity and link to research; they are not physics simulations or emergency training.

## 6. Show the engineering

Open the public repository's architecture diagram, semantic-search documentation, snapshot audit and recorded probe. Explain that the vector database helps discover related meanings while structured source rows retain exact values. The reproducible reconstruction can run locally without model calls.
